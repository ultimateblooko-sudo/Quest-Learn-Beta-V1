Questions, answered.

What is Quest Learn?

A learning platform that turns your courses and extracurriculars into skill nodes, then uses Apex AI to create new ones based on your progression.

How does Apex AI build my path?

You enter your courses and extracurriculars. Apex AI maps each into skill nodes and schedules them on your calendar. As your skills develop, it creates new nodes to continue your progress.

Does it work for sports, music and clubs?

Yes. Training, practice and club work are mapped into nodes alongside your courses and share the same calendar.

How is it different from a planner?

A planner stores the tasks you give it. Quest Learn creates your nodes, flashcards and calendar, and adapts them as your skills progress.

Is my information private?

What you enter is used to build and adapt your learning path. Full privacy details will be published before launch.
