Questions, answered.

What is Quest Learn?

A learning app that reads your classes and your extracurriculars, then builds one personalized course around both.

How does it build my course?

You add your classes and what you do outside school. Quest Learn maps each subject into small checkpoints and places them in your week.

Does it work for sports, music and clubs?

Yes. Training, practice and club work sit in the same plan as your classes, so nothing competes for the same hour.

How is it different from a planner?

A planner stores your tasks. Quest Learn creates the lessons, flashcards and checkpoints, and adapts them as you progress.

Is my information private?

What you enter is used to build your course. Full privacy details will be published before launch.
